# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: a wholesale flower buyer (the owner) who buys flowers from farmers by weight and pays them. Uses it on a phone at the weighing point and on a laptop when reviewing. Reads Tamil or English. A separate technical team runs the backend, data security and network protection.

## Product Purpose

Record every flower purchase from a farmer in five fields (date, flower, weight, price per kg, amount), see the day's bills at a glance, and audit every 15 days that each farmer's location has been checked, with a map and simple charts. Success: an entry takes under 15 seconds and the 15-day audit is understood without explanation.

## Brand Personality

Plain, trustworthy, calm. Reads like a well-kept ledger, not an enterprise console. Existing green Fresh Blooms identity is kept.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise admin dashboards with many KPI cards, sidebars and nested menus (the previous version of this app).
- Marketing-style hero metrics, gradients, decorative motion.
- Machine-translated Tamil that reads unnaturally.

## Design Principles

1. One page, one primary action: record the purchase.
2. Smart defaults over questions: today's date, last flower and price stay filled; amount is calculated.
3. Every number is checkable: totals, charts and the table agree and come from the same data.
4. Language is first-class: every visible string switches between Tamil and English, including statuses, flowers, errors and dates.
5. Works the same on a phone and a laptop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, touch targets at least 44px, numbers large and legible outdoors, status never shown by color alone (icon + label), keyboard usable on laptop, reduced motion respected, Tamil font with proper glyph coverage.
